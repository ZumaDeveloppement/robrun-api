# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, welcome newcomers, accept constructive feedback, and focus on what is best for the community.
Harassment, insults, and discrimination are not tolerated.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer **Zuma.Eu** through the Discord server:
https://discord.gg/rejoins (open a private ticket or message a moderator). Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, issues, and contributions that do not follow this code, and may ban
contributors who repeatedly break it.
